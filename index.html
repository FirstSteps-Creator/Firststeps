About the Initiative

Co-sponsored care for people in need

First Steps is a co-sponsored initiative working with legal aid organizations, shelters, and community partners to remove barriers to stability. Our goal is to ensure everyone has access to the documents and support needed to move forward.

Accessible help

One-on-one guidance helps participants complete forms, attend appointments, and stay informed at every step.

Trusted partnerships

We collaborate with local nonprofits, legal clinics, and public agencies to create stronger referral paths.

Empowering independence

Beyond paperwork, we focus on life skills, job readiness, and connection to stable housing.

What we offer

Support services designed for every stage

Legal Documentation

Assistance with IDs, SSNs, birth certificates, and legal records so people can access services, work, and housing.

Resource Connections

Direct referrals to shelters, food programs, medical care, mental health support, and employment resources.

Independence Planning

Workshops, coaching, and follow-up help participants create stable plans for housing, budgeting, and long-term success.
