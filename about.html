About First Steps

First Steps is a community initiative dedicated to helping individuals experiencing homelessness regain stability, access identification, connect with essential services, and move toward independence.

We believe every person deserves dignity, support, and an opportunity to rebuild their future.

Our Mission

We work to remove barriers by helping individuals obtain IDs, birth certificates, transportation assistance, shelter referrals, and connections to local resources.

What We Do

Our volunteers assist with outreach events, paperwork support, appointment scheduling, and resource navigation throughout the community.

Why It Matters

Access to identification and transportation can be life-changing. First Steps helps people take the first step toward housing, employment, healthcare, and long-term stability.
