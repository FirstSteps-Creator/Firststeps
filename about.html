About First Steps

First Steps Initiative supports individuals experiencing homelessness by helping them access identification assistance, transportation resources, shelter referrals, and community support.

Our goal is to help people build stability, independence, and hope through compassionate outreach and practical assistance.

Join Our Mission

Our Mission

We work to remove barriers that prevent individuals from accessing housing, employment, healthcare, and stability.

Community Support

Volunteers help with outreach events, transportation, paperwork assistance, and connecting people with local services.

Why It Matters

Small steps can change lives. Access to IDs, transportation, and support services often becomes the foundation for long-term success.
