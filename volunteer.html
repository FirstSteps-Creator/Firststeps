Join Our Team

Make a direct impact in your community. Whether you want to assist with administrative support, help clients gather vital documents, or mentor individuals navigating milestones, we have a place for your talents.

Volunteer Roles


• Document Navigators: Assist clients directly during check-ins with obtaining vital birth certificates, dynamic state IDs, and records processing.


• Operations Support: Work quietly in the background handling local resource sorting, technical data tracking, or site coordination layouts.


• Flexible Scheduling: We work around your availability. Whether you can give 2 hours a month or 10 hours a week, every moment helps.



Requirements

• Must be 18 years or older.

• Must complete a basic background screening orientation.

• Compassion, kindness, and patience for all neighbors.
