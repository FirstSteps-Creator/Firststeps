Join the Initiative

Volunteers are the heartbeat of the First Steps Initiative. Whether you are helping an individual navigate legal documentation, helping with community outreach events, or organizing resource drives, your time directly creates pathways toward independence for our unhoused neighbors.

Ways You Can Make an Impact


• Document Navigators: Assist individuals with the administrative steps of filling out paperwork for state IDs, birth certificates, and security documents.


• Event Support: Help coordinate, set up, and manage community outreach drives, food donations, and clothing distribution centers.


• Resource Drivers: Provide crucial transportation assistance to ensure individuals can safely get to essential medical, legal, or shelter appointments.
